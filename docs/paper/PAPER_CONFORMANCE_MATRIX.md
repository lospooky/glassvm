## Result and limits

No claim-breaking difference in the shared publication-facing contract was
found among the three machines. Their declared artifacts, machine
configurations, input surfaces, native evidence, and emulator semantics remain
bundle-owned and materially different. In particular, PICO-8 and TIC-80 are
Lua compatibility-runtime specimens with documented subsets, not instruction-
exact or complete-console implementations. CHIP-8 remains the baseline rather
than a proxy for those semantics.

The matrix establishes contract and conformance depth, not full compatibility,
cross-platform wheel support, benchmark performance, or run-length-independent
memory. The Python package distributions are not yet published to PyPI. The
separate machine-heterogeneity table and all empirical paper claims remain
later slices.
